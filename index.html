The problem

Managers lead their teams without knowing what motivates each person, and nobody can see when someone is struggling (stressed, burning out, or losing interest). People managed this way disengage, but they rarely resign: they stay and underperform, seven in ten employees in the UAE and eight in ten worldwide, a loss most organizations discover only from a survey a year later.

How it works

Every employee is on it. Managers use it daily. HR sees the aggregate.

A motivation profile

Each employee completes a validated motivation profile once. They see their own results and guidance.

A two-tap weekly check-in

A short check-in each week keeps the picture current and shows when something changes.

Guidance for the manager

Managers get the team view: what motivates each person, what to do this week, and who needs attention.

Where AI fits. A certified rule base, built from years of consulting practice, decides the guidance. AI writes it in plain words. The model is swappable, including Arabic-native models.

Built for trust

Motivation data is personal. The platform is designed so organizations can answer employees' questions before they are asked.

  • Employees opt in, and see their own profile and guidance.
  • Visibility settings let each organization decide what managers see.
  • HR sees team and organization trends, not individual check-ins.
  • The guidance comes from rules you can inspect, not from a black box.
